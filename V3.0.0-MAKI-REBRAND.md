## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### Goals
1. **Delightful** - Make every step feel good
2. **Clear** - Easy to understand what's happening
3. **Professional** - Beautiful but not distracting
4. **Consistent** - Same visual language throughout
5. **Fun** - Emoji and personality without being silly

### Color Scheme
- **Magenta** - Primary brand color, headers, CTAs
- **Cyan** - Secondary actions, info, commands
- **Green** - Success states, confirmation
- **Yellow** - Warnings, tips, important info
- **Red** - Errors, problems

### Typography Hierarchy
1. **ASCII Art** - Brand identity (figlet)
2. **Boxed Headers** - Major sections (boxen)
3. **Gradient Text** - Celebrations (gradient-string)
4. **Bold Text** - Section titles (chalk.bold)
5. **Regular** - Body text (chalk)
6. **Dim** - Helper text (chalk.dim)
